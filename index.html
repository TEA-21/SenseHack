05 / Need a signal boost?
Questions?
We've got you.
Browse the quick answers or send the team a question.
When and where is SenseHack '26?
December 9–10, 2026 at Anna Auditorium, VIT Vellore. The event runs for 36 hours.
Who can participate?
Students from VIT Vellore and other colleges can participate. Follow the registration route for your category.
How many tracks are there?
There are seven tracks: Smart Healthcare, Smart Cities, Digital Education, Fintech, Green Tech, Cybersecurity and Open Innovation.
Can I request hardware?
Yes. Use the separate hardware registration form for your category. Submit your requirements clearly and accurately.
What should I bring?
Bring your student identification, laptop, chargers and any personal essentials required for a 36-hour build event. Official instructions will be shared with registered teams.
Where can I ask something not listed here?
Use the query form alongside this FAQ. The organising team can follow up by email.
