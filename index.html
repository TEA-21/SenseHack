05 / Knowledge Base & Queries


Clear Answers · Direct Inquiries
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Browse the accordion answers on the left or send an instant query to our organizing team on the right.











When and where is SenseHack '26?

SenseHack '26 takes place on December 9–10, 2026, at Anna Auditorium, VIT Vellore campus. The hackathon runs continuously for 36 hours from the opening ceremony on Day 1 through the final evaluations and valedictory on Day 2.






Who can participate and what is the team size?

The hackathon is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students from VIT Vellore and colleges nationwide. Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Inter-disciplinary teams pairing hardware builders with software developers are warmly encouraged.






How does registration differ for VIT vs Non-VIT students?

VIT Vellore students register directly using the internal Event Registration Google Form and submit lab hardware requests via the internal Hardware form. Non-VIT students must first register through the VIT Events Website (events.vit.ac.in) for campus accreditation, followed by completing the external Event and Hardware Google Forms.






How does the hardware requisition process work?

Teams requiring physical components (microcontrollers, sensor breakouts, communication modules, development boards) must specify their kit requirements in the Hardware Registration Form. Components will be pre-allocated and distributed at your assigned team workbench inside Anna Auditorium.






What are the 7 competition tracks?

The 7 tracks are: Smart Healthcare, Smart Cities & IoT, Digital Education, Fintech & Web3, Green & Sustainable Tech, Cybersecurity & Defense, and Open Innovation. Detailed problem statements and rubrics will be unveiled during the opening briefing.






What facilities and support are provided during the 36 hours?

All participants are provided with designated workstations, high-speed campus WiFi, continuous power backup, all meals, snacks, midnight coffee, resting lounges, mentor office hours, and official participant merchandise.
