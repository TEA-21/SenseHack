04 / Knowledge Base & Queries


Clear Answers · Direct Inquiries
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS










When and where is SenseHack '26?

SenseHack '26 takes place on December 9–10, 2026, at Anna Auditorium, VIT Vellore campus. The hackathon runs continuously for 36 hours from the opening ceremony on Day 1 through the final evaluations and valedictory on Day 2.






Who can participate and what is the team size?

The hackathon is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students from VIT Vellore and colleges nationwide. Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Inter-disciplinary teams pairing hardware builders with software developers are warmly encouraged.






How does registration differ for internal and external participants?

Internal participants (VIT Vellore students) register through the Event Registration Google Form. External participants (students from other colleges) register on the VIT Events website at events.vit.ac.in and through the Registration Google Form. Hardware registration for both groups is coming soon and will be announced here.






How does the hardware requisition process work?

Teams requiring physical components (microcontrollers, sensor breakouts, communication modules, development boards) will be able to specify their kit requirements in the Hardware Registration, which is coming soon. Components will be pre-allocated and distributed at your assigned team workbench inside Anna Auditorium.






What are the 7 competition tracks?

The 7 tracks are: Smart Healthcare, Smart Cities, Digital Education, Fintech, Green Tech, Cybersecurity, and Open Innovation. Detailed problem statements and rubrics will be unveiled during the opening briefing.






What facilities and support are provided during the 36 hours?

All participants are provided with designated workstations, high-speed campus WiFi, continuous power backup, all meals, snacks, midnight coffee, resting lounges, mentor office hours, and official participant merchandise.
